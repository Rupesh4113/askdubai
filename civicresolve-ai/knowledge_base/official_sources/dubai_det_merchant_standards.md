**Authority**: Dubai DET Commercial Compliance Division

## Consumer Protection Standards for Commercial Outlets
1. **Mandatory Bilingual Receipt**:
   Every merchant operating in Dubai must issue a receipt printed in Arabic and English. The receipt must clearly state the merchant's trade name, TRN, date, item, price in UAE Dirhams (AED), and store return/exchange policy.
2. **"No Return, No Exchange" Clauses Prohibited**:
   Clauses stating "Goods once sold cannot be returned or exchanged" are legally null and void if the good is found to be defective, counterfeit, non-conforming to technical standards, or unfit for purpose.
3. **Air Conditioning & Home Appliance Specific Standards**:
   - Authorized dealers must provide manufacturer warranty cards stamped with purchase date.
   - For air conditioning units installed by the retailer or certified technician, warranty covers compressor for five (5) years and full unit parts/labor for minimum two (2) years.
   - If an air conditioning unit ceases cooling or leaks refrigerant within 14 days of installation due to manufacturing or certified installation error, dealer must replace unit within 7 business days or refund in full.
4. **Resolution Deadlines**:
   - Merchants must respond to formal customer service complaints within 48 hours.
   - If the merchant fails to reach an amicable settlement, the consumer may submit case to Dubai Consumer Rights CCCP.
