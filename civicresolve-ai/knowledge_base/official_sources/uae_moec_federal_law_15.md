# UAE Federal Law No. 15 of 2020 on Consumer Protection & Executive Regulations
**Source**: UAE Ministry of Economy (MOEC)
**Official URL**: https://www.moec.gov.ae/en/consumer-protection
**Domain**: moec.gov.ae
**Verified Date**: 2026-01-10
**Authority**: Ministry of Economy, United Arab Emirates
**Jurisdiction**: Federal UAE (All Emirates)

## Legislative Overview
Federal Law No. 15 of 2020 replaced the earlier Federal Law No. 24 of 2006, expanding statutory protections to cover electronic commerce, digital services, data privacy, fair contract terms, and rapid dispute settlement. The Executive Regulations enacted under Cabinet Decision No. 66 of 2023 set enforceable operational rules across all retail and service sectors in the United Arab Emirates.

## Article 4 & 5: Fundamental Consumer Rights
1. Right to Safety: Protection against products, production processes, and services that endanger consumer health or safety.
2. Right to Knowledge: Right to receive accurate, transparent, and complete information regarding goods and services, including genuine country of origin, specifications, operating manuals, and clear pricing in AED.
3. Right to Redress and Fair Compensation: Right to prompt settlement of legitimate claims, repair of defective items, replacement, or restitution of financial damages resulting from defective or substandard goods.

## Article 10: Defective Goods, Malfunctions, and Supplier Liability
- The supplier is legally liable for any manufacturing defect, lack of conformity, or performance failure that renders the good unfit for its customary use.
- Upon discovery of a defect within the statutory warranty or reasonable operational lifetime:
  1. The consumer has the legal right to demand the repair of the good, replacement with an identical or superior compliant good, or return of the purchase price against return of the defective item.
  2. If the defect is fundamental, critical to safety, or cannot be repaired within the regulatory period (maximum 14 to 21 calendar days), the supplier must issue an immediate refund or replacement without charging depreciation.

## Article 12: Warranty Obligations and After-Sales Maintenance
- Suppliers, distributors, and agents are legally obliged to provide after-sales servicing, warranty repairs, and periodic maintenance throughout the expected economic lifetime of the product.
- Mandatory spare parts must be stocked for consumer durables (vehicles, air conditioners, major electrical appliances) by authorized distributors for a minimum of 5 years or the product life cycle.

## Article 17: Prohibited Deceptive Commercial Conduct
- Advertising or packaging containing fraudulent, exaggerated, or misleading claims is prohibited.
- Hidden costs, compulsory bundling of unwanted auxiliary warranties without affirmative consumer consent, and unilateral alterations of agreed terms are unlawful.

## Article 25: Penalties for Non-Compliance
- Failure to comply with consumer protection directives or refusing lawful refunds exposes the offending merchant to administrative fines ranging from AED 10,000 up to AED 1,000,000, temporary license suspension, or business closure ordered by the competent authority.
