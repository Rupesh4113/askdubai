# Dubai Department of Economy and Tourism (DET) - Consumer Rights Manual
**Source**: Department of Economy and Tourism (DET) - Commercial Compliance & Consumer Protection (CCCP)
**Official URL**: https://consumerrights.gov.ae/
**Domain**: consumerrights.gov.ae
**Verified Date**: 2026-01-15
**Authority**: Dubai Department of Economy and Tourism
**Jurisdiction**: Dubai Emirate (Commercial entities, retail stores, local e-commerce, home appliances, automotive, electronics)

## 1. Statutory Warranty and Defective Goods in Dubai
Under Dubai Consumer Protection guidelines and UAE Federal Law:
- All household appliances, including air conditioners, refrigerators, washing machines, and electronic goods, carry a mandatory legal warranty from the authorized dealer or retailer of at least one (1) to two (2) years.
- If an appliance fails or exhibits a major manufacturing defect within fourteen (14) days from the date of purchase or installation, the consumer is legally entitled to:
  1. An immediate replacement with a brand new unit of identical specifications, or
  2. A complete full refund of the purchase price without deduction, returned using the original payment method.
- If a defect occurs after fourteen (14) days but within the warranty period:
  - The supplier is obligated to repair the defective item free of charge.
  - Repairs must not exceed fifteen (15) to twenty-one (21) calendar days.
  - If parts are unavailable or the repair cannot be completed within 21 days, or if the unit suffers the same defect three (3) consecutive times, the merchant must replace the unit or issue a full refund.
  - The merchant must provide a temporary loaner replacement for essential appliances (such as air conditioners in summer or refrigerators) during extended repairs.

## 2. Invoices and Proof of Purchase
- Merchants in Dubai are legally mandated to issue bilingual (Arabic and English) tax invoices for every commercial transaction.
- The invoice must state the merchant trade name, date of purchase, invoice number, itemized description, quantities, unit price, VAT breakdown, and return/warranty conditions.
- Failure of a merchant to issue an official invoice is a regulatory violation subject to administrative fines.

## 3. Delayed Delivery and Non-Performance
- When a delivery date is agreed upon (verbal or written), the supplier must fulfill delivery within the stipulated window.
- In case of a merchant delay exceeding seven (7) days without consumer consent:
  - The consumer has the legal right to cancel the purchase agreement and receive a full immediate refund.
  - The supplier cannot impose cancellation or restocking penalties when the delay was caused by the merchant.

## 4. Misleading Advertising and Pricing Discrepancies
- The displayed price on shelf, website, or promotional flyer must match the price charged at checkout.
- If a discrepancy exists, the merchant is legally bound to honor the lower displayed price.
- Misleading promotions, fake discounts, and bait-and-switch advertising are strictly prohibited under Article 17 of UAE Law.

## 5. Official Complaint Filing Procedure in Dubai
- **Step 1: Direct Merchant Escalation**: The consumer should first submit a formal written complaint (via email, customer service ticket, or registered message) to the merchant, giving a 7-day cure window.
- **Step 2: Filing with Dubai DED (Consumer Rights)**: If the merchant is unresponsive or refuses resolution:
  - Portal: https://consumerrights.gov.ae/en/Pages/consumer-complaint.aspx
  - Dubai Consumer Smart App (iOS / Android)
  - Toll-free Helpline: 600 545555
- **Required Documents**:
  1. Copy of the Tax Invoice / Receipt.
  2. Copy of Warranty Certificate / Card.
  3. Evidence of communication with merchant (emails, chat logs, call reference numbers).
  4. Photographs or video documentation showing the defect or model serial number.
  5. Emirates ID or Resident Passport copy of the complainant.
- **Resolution Timeline**: Dubai DED initiates mediation within four (4) business days and resolves typical retail disputes within seven (7) to fourteen (14) business days.
