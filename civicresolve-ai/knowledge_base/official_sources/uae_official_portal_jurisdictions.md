## Statutory Consumer Dispute Jurisdiction Matrix
Filing with the correct government body ensures immediate jurisdiction and avoids administrative rejection.

### 1. Dubai Department of Economy and Tourism (DET / DED)
- **Scope**: Commercial purchases, retail stores, local service providers, restaurants, supermarkets, home appliances (ACs, electronics), Dubai-licensed e-commerce vendors.
- **Authority**: Commercial Compliance & Consumer Protection (CCCP).
- **Portal**: https://consumerrights.gov.ae/
- **Contact**: 600 545555.

### 2. UAE Ministry of Economy (MOEC)
- **Scope**: Cross-emirate commercial disputes, national anti-monopoly violations, pricing disputes on basic food commodities, businesses operating without emirate-level consumer bureaus.
- **Portal**: https://www.moec.gov.ae/en/consumer-protection
- **Contact**: 800 1222.

### 3. Telecommunications and Digital Government Regulatory Authority (TDRA)
- **Scope**: Mobile network operators (e& / Etisalat, du, Virgin Mobile UAE), fixed broadband, cellular contract billing, sim swaps, data bundle disputes, postal delivery services (Emirates Post).
- **Condition**: Consumer must first obtain a formal complaint reference number from the telecom provider and wait five (5) working days before escalating to TDRA.
- **Portal**: https://tdra.gov.ae/en/services/consumer-complaints
- **Contact**: 800 12.

### 4. Sanadak - Financial & Insurance Ombudsman (Central Bank of UAE)
- **Scope**: Commercial banks, Islamic financial institutions, insurance companies, credit card unauthorized debits, mortgage disputes, consumer credit claims.
- **Eligibility**: Independent Ombudsman established by federal decree to deliver binding rulings up to statutory limits.
- **Portal**: https://sanadak.gov.ae/
- **Contact**: 800 7262325 (800 SANADAK).

### 5. Dubai Land Department (DLD / RERA)
- **Scope**: Real estate tenant-landlord disputes, property developer maintenance escrow, rental increases contrary to the RERA calculator.
- **Portal**: https://dubailand.gov.ae/

## Consumer Redress Checklist
1. Purchase Proof: Valid Tax Invoice indicating TRN (Tax Registration Number) and merchant name.
2. Timeline of Incident: Documenting date of purchase, date of failure, and merchant notification date.
3. Merchant Response Record: Copies of emails, support tickets, or registered SMS notices.
4. Objective Redress Specification: Clearly stating whether requesting repair, replacement, or refund.
