# CivicResolve AI - Dubai Consumer Research Questionnaire
### Structured Field Interview Protocol for 30–50 Dubai Residents

**Objective**: Gather empirical qualitative and quantitative data regarding consumer dispute experiences, language friction, document management, and willingness to adopt AI-assisted resolution in Dubai.

---

## Participant Screener
- **Location**: Dubai Resident (Citizen, Resident Visa Holder, or Golden Visa Holder).
- **Time in UAE**: Minimum 6 months.
- **Retail Activity**: Purchased at least one major consumer good (electronics, home appliance, furniture, car repair) or used e-commerce (Noon, Amazon, retail apps) in the past 12 months.
- **Target Sample**: 40 Participants (8 UAE Citizens, 12 South Asian Expatriates, 8 Arab Expatriates, 6 Western/European Expatriates, 6 Southeast Asian Expatriates).

---

## Part 1: Consumer Dispute Experience (Quantitative)

1. **In the past 24 months, have you encountered any of the following commercial issues in Dubai?** *(Select all that apply)*
   - [ ] Defective product or appliance failing prematurely (e.g. AC, phone, fridge, TV).
   - [ ] Retailer or authorized service center refusing warranty repair.
   - [ ] Merchant refusing an authorized refund or replacement.
   - [ ] Delayed online delivery exceeding promised arrival date by >7 days.
   - [ ] Misleading price tag or hidden fee charged at checkout.
   - [ ] Unauthorized debit card or credit card billing charge.
   - [ ] None of the above.

2. **If you experienced an issue, what was the estimated monetary value involved?**
   - [ ] Less than AED 500
   - [ ] AED 500 – AED 2,000
   - [ ] AED 2,000 – AED 5,000
   - [ ] Greater than AED 5,000

3. **How did you first attempt to resolve the issue?**
   - [ ] Spoke directly with customer service in-store.
   - [ ] Sent emails / WhatsApp messages to the merchant.
   - [ ] Called the merchant call center.
   - [ ] Gave up because of time/effort friction.
   - [ ] Immediately filed with Dubai Consumer Rights (DED CCCP).

---

## Part 2: Friction Points & Service Gaps (Qualitative)

4. **What was the single most frustrating aspect of trying to resolve your dispute?**
   - [ ] Merchant being unresponsive or giving repetitive automated replies.
   - [ ] Losing the printed paper receipt or warranty card.
   - [ ] Unclear understanding of what UAE law actually entitles you to (repair vs refund vs replacement).
   - [ ] Not knowing which government authority had jurisdiction over the merchant.
   - [ ] Language barrier (difficulty explaining technical or legal issues in Arabic or English).

5. **Did you know that under UAE Federal Law No. 15 of 2020 and Dubai DET standards, suppliers are legally mandated to repair defective appliances within 14–21 days, or provide an immediate replacement or full refund if defective within the statutory window?**
   - [ ] Yes, I knew this specific legal timeline.
   - [ ] I assumed there was a law, but didn't know the exact rules or deadlines.
   - [ ] No, I had no idea.

6. **Have you ever used the official Dubai Consumer Rights portal (`consumerrights.gov.ae`) or app?**
   - [ ] Yes, filed a complaint successfully.
   - [ ] Visited the website but did not finish filing.
   - [ ] Never heard of it / didn't know how to file.

---

## Part 3: AI Intervention & CivicResolve Adoption

7. **If an intelligent AI assistant could inspect your receipt photo, determine your exact legal rights under Dubai law, draft a formal legal demand letter to the merchant, and prepare your official government complaint in under 3 minutes, would you use it?**
   - [ ] Definitely yes
   - [ ] Probably yes
   - [ ] Unsure
   - [ ] No

8. **In what language would you prefer the AI assistant to communicate with you?**
   - [ ] English
   - [ ] Arabic (العربية)
   - [ ] Hindi (हिन्दी)
   - [ ] Urdu (اردو)
   - [ ] Malayalam (മലയാളം)
   - [ ] Tagalog / Filipino
   - [ ] Other

9. **What privacy assurance is most essential for you before uploading an invoice or receipt?**
   - [ ] Explicit guarantee that bank account passwords or OTPs are never requested.
   - [ ] Confirmation that receipts are stored securely and never shared with advertisers.
   - [ ] Requirement that the system asks for my explicit approval before sending anything externally.
